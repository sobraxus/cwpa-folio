About

A SOC analyst with a degree in cyber security and a home lab that never quite stays finished

I am Generic, based in the UK and working remotely. I trained at the University of Genericness on the BSc Cyber Security and Forensic Computing course. The work I care about sits between a noisy alert and a decision someone can stand behind.

I keep myself current with labs, TryHackMe, and a personal home-lab repo for Python, PowerShell, incident-response notes, and anything else I pick up while practising. I want to help make the environment around a queue a little more secure, even when that work is quiet.

Portfolio

Work I keep coming back to

Threat Detection

Finding the signal in a noisy queue. I look at what actually fired, what the logs say, and whether it is worth a person's time.

Alert Triage

Deciding what to open, what to enrich, and what to close. Most of the day is this step. I want it faster and more honest.

Home Lab

A personal repo for reusable notes and scripts: Python, PowerShell, C, regex, incident-response write-ups, and TryHackMe labs.

Incident Notes

Practice for the response step after an alert is worth opening. Notes, timelines, and the habit of writing down what actually happened.

Log Analysis

Reading what the systems said, not what the ticket title guessed. Linux, query work, and enough scripting to repeat a check tomorrow.
