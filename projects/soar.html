SOAR

Security orchestration, automation, and response. I am building the path from an alert that deserves attention, through context, to a decision and an action — without making the analyst the glue in the middle.

the specifics land here as each piece is ready to show.

The shape of it

01 Detect

Keep the alerts that are worth an analyst's time. Drop the ones that only make the queue longer.

02 Enrich

Put context on the case before anyone opens it: what it is, where it sits, what has already been seen.

03 Decide

A triage path a person can follow. The point of the summary is a decision, not another paragraph.

04 Respond

The action, then a record of it. This is the step still being shaped.

What is public

This page is the shareable version. Playbooks, detections, and lab notes stay off it until I decide a piece is safe and finished enough to show. Related work that is already public: TriageRPG, which prototypes the analyst side of triage, and the 2024 SIEM project on false positives.

Where it sits

TriageRPG covers the case a person looks at. This project is the orchestration around that case: what fires, what gets attached, and what is allowed to happen next. They are meant to meet in the middle.
