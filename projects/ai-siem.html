AI SIEM

Artificially intelligent SIEM for targeted threat detection and analysis. A final-year project at the University of Portsmouth, on the BSc Cyber Security and Forensic Computing course.

queue

The aim was fewer false positives for the person triaging.

The question

False positives are where a SOC spends time it does not have. The project asked whether federated learning — logistic regression and FedAvg, with scikit-learn and Flower — could take a bite out of that, using the UNSW-NB15 dataset from the University of New South Wales.

What actually happened

Software, hardware, and time pushed the plan off its first line. The logistic regression model performed moderately. A high loss, likely because the model had been overfit, held the result back. The write-up treats that as the finding: federated learning belongs in this problem, and it needs a stronger model, something like a quantile regression forest or a neural net, plus TensorFlow and a GPU, before anyone should trust it in a queue.
