TriageRPG

A SOC analyst dashboard that treats the backlog like a campaign. Closing a case is the progress. The first screen you get is a summary, not an empty ticket.

progressXP per case

first readAI summary

What it does

Cases are meant to arrive from the tools that already raise them, such as a webhook from Splunk or Jira. Sign in with GitHub, open a case, and Gemini writes a triage summary. A seed script can drop a dummy case in so the screen can be tested before anything real is connected.

Why it exists

The SOAR work is about what happens around a case. TriageRPG is the case itself: the thing an analyst looks at, finishes, and gets credit for finishing. One is orchestration. This one is the desk.
